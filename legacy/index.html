About Me

I'm Keshav Singh, a Computer Science engineering student building at the intersection of full-stack web development, applied Generative AI, and Web3.

On the product side I ship modern apps with Next.js, React, TypeScript and FastAPI. On the AI side I build RAG pipelines, AI agents and multi-agent systems using LangChain, LlamaIndex, Ollama and models from OpenAI and Google Gemini.

I'm also deep into Web3 — writing smart contracts in Solidity, integrating wallets and DeFi, and wiring up live crypto market data — always with clean architecture and real-world impact.
